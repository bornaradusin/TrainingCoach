# Plans

Plans that come with the app, all made for the IM-309:

| Plan | How often |
|---|---|
| **Beginner full body** | One workout for the whole body, 3 times a week |
| **Full body A / B** | Two full-body workouts in turn, 3 times a week |
| **Upper / Lower** | 4 days: upper, lower and core, upper, lower and core |
| **Push / Pull / Legs** | 3 days in turn, 3 to 6 times a week |

Tap a plan to see its days, then **Use this plan**. You can also start any of its days straight away.

## Your own plans

**Make your own plan**, or open a plan and tap **Copy and change**. Give it days, add exercises to each day, and set the sets, the rep range and the rest for each one. Your plans can be changed or deleted at any time; workouts you did with them stay in your history.
