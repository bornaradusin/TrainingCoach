# Settings

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/settings.png" width="260" alt="Settings"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/settings-stack.png" width="260" alt="Weight stack">

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Units** | kg or lb everywhere |
| **Weight stack** | The weight at each pin. The IM-309 has a 3.5 kg top plate and 11 plates of 5.7 kg (145 lb in all), so pin 1 is 3.5 kg and each pin down adds 5.7 kg, up to 66.2 kg. **Change weights** if your machine differs; **Back to IM-309** resets them. |
| **Rest timer** | The rest for exercises you add to a workout (plans have their own), and whether it vibrates and beeps |
| **Keep the screen on during a workout** | On by default |

**Updates**: **Check for updates** looks at the newest release on GitHub, and installs it after checking it against the release's SHA256SUMS.txt.
