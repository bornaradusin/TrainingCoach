# Progress

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/progress.png" width="260" alt="Progress"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/exercise-progress.png" width="260" alt="An exercise's progress">

**Progress** shows your workouts, sets and how much you've lifted in all, then every exercise you've done with your best set and a small chart.

Tap an exercise for:
- its **weight** over time (the best set of each workout)
- the **estimated one-rep max** (from weight and reps), which goes up when either does
- every workout's sets

Under the exercises is every workout you've done; tap one to see its sets or delete it.
