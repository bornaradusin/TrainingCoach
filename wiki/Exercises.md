# Exercises

The **Exercises** tab has every exercise the IM-309 can do, by station:

- **Press arms**: chest press, butterfly (pec deck), reverse butterfly
- **Top pulley**: lat pulldown, close-grip pulldown, straight-arm pulldown, triceps pushdown, rope pushdown, kneeling cable crunch
- **Low pulley**: preacher curl, standing cable curl, upright row, cable shrug, seated low row, front raise
- **Backrest pulley**: seated cable crunch
- **Leg station**: leg extension, standing leg curl
- **Bodyweight**: sit-up with your feet under the leg roller

Tap one to see:
- **An animation** of it done on the machine, with the cable and the weight stack moving: the lift, a pause, and the slower way back.
- **Setting up**: which pulley and attachment, the seat, where your feet go.
- **How to do it**, step by step, and **tips** for good form.
- What it works, and the suggested reps.
- **Your progress** on it, once you've done it.

The small **YouTube** button under the animation searches YouTube for that exercise, if you want to see a person do it.
