# Training

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/workout.png" width="260" alt="Workout"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/trainingcoach/images/rest.png" width="260" alt="Rest timer">

On **Train**, the next day of your plan is ready. Tap the day's name chips to do a different one, then **Start**.

During a workout:
- The chips at the top are the day's exercises; ✓ marks the done ones. **+ Add** puts another exercise in this workout.
- The card shows the exercise's animation, its attachment and the target. **How to** opens its instructions; **Swap** changes it for another exercise.
- **Pin**: where to put the pin in the stack, with its weight. Under it the app says why it suggests that pin.
- **Reps**: what you did. Then **Log set**.
- The **rest timer** starts after each set (from the plan; **+30 s** or **Skip rest**). It buzzes and beeps when the rest is over.
- When an exercise's sets are done, the next one comes up. **Skip** leaves one out.
- **Finish** saves the workout. Closing the app doesn't lose it: **Continue** on Train picks it up again.

The screen stays on during a workout (it can be turned off in Settings).

## The suggested pin

The app uses double progression:
- Did the **top of the rep range on every set** last time? It suggests **one plate more** (the next pin down).
- Below the **bottom of the range on every set**? **One plate less**.
- Otherwise the **same pin**, aiming for the top of the range.
